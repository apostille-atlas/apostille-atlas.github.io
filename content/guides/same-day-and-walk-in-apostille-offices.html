If you need an apostille fast, going in person is usually the quickest official route. This page lists what each state office we cover says about walk-in and same-day service, taken from its own official page. Ohio and the federal office are included too.

Which states offer same-day or walk-in apostilles?

Where can I get a same-day apostille?

  • California: same-day service at the Sacramento office (1500 11th Street, 3rd Floor) and the Los Angeles office (300 South Spring Street, no appointments, take a number), and at Apostille Pop-Up Shops the office runs with county offices. Upcoming pop-up dates listed on the official page include San Bernardino (November 10, 2026), South Lake Tahoe (November 16), San Diego (December 2) and Richmond. In person, California adds a $6 special handling fee for each different official's signature on top of the $20 apostille fee (California SOS).
  • New York: same-day walk-in service in New York City, Albany, Binghamton, Buffalo and Utica, limited to 10 documents per customer (NY Department of State).
  • Texas: same-day service at the Austin office, 10 documents maximum per person, company or transaction. Walk in on Monday or Friday; Tuesday to Thursday need an appointment (one per person or company per day). More than 10 documents can go in the drop box, processed within 72 hours (Texas SOS).
  • Illinois: in person in Springfield, Chicago and select DMV+ locations; the state says most in-person requests are processed while you wait (Illinois SOS).
  • Pennsylvania: walk-ins 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, processed while you wait. The office asks you to arrive by 4 p.m. (PA Department of State).
  • Georgia: walk-in 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, no appointment; the office says it typically takes less than twenty minutes, depending on the number of documents (GSCCCA).

Which offices take documents in person but don't promise same-day service?

  • Arizona: walk-in service with a $25 expedite fee, limit 6 documents. From November 2, 2026, the Phoenix office is by appointment only, booked online, and in-person service moves away from the 1700 W. Washington St. Executive Tower; the location is given when you book. Tucson: 400 West Congress, Suite 221 (Arizona SOS).
  • North Carolina: documents can be delivered in person; the state handles emergencies case by case if you send documentation of the emergency (NC SOS).
  • New Jersey: order online first, then mail or drop off your documents with the confirmation page (NJ Division of Revenue).
  • Ohio: the request form says you may deliver documents in person if you need faster service, but no same-day turnaround is published (Ohio Form 8003).
  • Florida: the official page does not describe walk-in service; requests are mailed (Florida DOS).

Can I get a same-day apostille for federal documents?

Only in an emergency. FBI background checks and other federal documents are apostilled by the U.S. Department of State, not by a state. Walk-in drop-offs in Washington, DC are processed within 7 business days, and same-day appointments are only for a life-or-death family emergency abroad with travel within 2 weeks. What should I check before going in person?

  • The document must be from the state whose office you visit. A New York birth certificate cannot be apostilled in Texas.
  • Check the document limit (10 in New York and Texas, 6 for Arizona's expedited walk-in) and whether you need an appointment that day.
  • Bring the fee in a form the office accepts; payment options are on each state page.
  • Some documents need a county or notary step first. The apostille FAQ and the birth certificate guide explain the common ones.
