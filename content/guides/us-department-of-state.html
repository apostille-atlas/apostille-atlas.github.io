If a federal agency or official signed your document (for example an FBI Identity History Summary or another federal record), the apostille comes from the U.S. Department of State Office of Authentications, not your state.

Fee$20 per document, non-refundable
FormDS-4194 (Request for Authentication Service)
By mailProcessed within 5 weeks of receipt (per the official page)
Walk-in drop-offProcessed within 7 business days. Drop-off is Monday–Thursday, 7:30–9:00 a.m., at 600 19th Street NW, Washington, DC 20006
Same-day appointmentOnly for a life-or-death family emergency with travel within 2 weeks
Mailing addressU.S. Department of State, Office of Authentications, 44132 Mercure Circle, PO Box 1206, Sterling, VA 20166-1206
PaymentBy mail: check or money order payable to the U.S. Department of State (no cash or card details). In person: credit or debit card, or contactless payment such as Apple Pay or Google Pay; no cash, checks or money orders.

Checklist

  1. Make sure your document is federal. State-issued documents (birth certificates, state court records, state-notarized papers) go to the state office instead.
  2. Fill out Form DS-4194.
  3. Include the $20 per-document fee.
  4. Mail the package to the Sterling, VA address, or drop it off in person during the walk-in window.
