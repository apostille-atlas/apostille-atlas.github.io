An FBI Identity History Summary is a federal document. State apostille offices cannot apostille it; Illinois and New York both say so on their official pages. The apostille comes from the U.S. Department of State Office of Authentications.

Steps

  1. Get your FBI Identity History Summary (from the FBI or an FBI-approved channeler).
  2. Complete Form DS-4194.
  3. Pay $20 per document (non-refundable). By mail, pay by check or money order.
  4. Mail it to: Office of Authentications, 44132 Mercure Circle, PO Box 1206, Sterling, VA 20166-1206. Or drop it off at 600 19th Street NW, Washington, DC (Mon–Thu, 7:30–9:00 a.m.).

Timing

The Department of State says it processes mailed requests within 5 weeks and walk-in drop-offs within 7 business days. Same-day service is only for a life-or-death family emergency with travel within 2 weeks.
