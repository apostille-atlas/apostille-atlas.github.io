## Lead-gen / referral-fee arrangements
- **Direct referral deals with apostille courier firms** that have no public program (e.g., the many DC embassy-legalization couriers). Would require outreach emails, so **NEEDS USER APPROVAL** per email. Drafts live in `outreach/`.
- **Attorneys:** do not sell leads or take referral fees from lawyers. ABA Model Rule 7.2(b) generally bars lawyers from paying for recommendations (with narrow exceptions), so this channel is risky for both sides. Source: https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_2_communications_concerning_a_lawyers_services_specific_rules/
